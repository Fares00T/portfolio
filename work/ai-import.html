Problem

Supplier catalogues come as PDFs, spreadsheets or websites, often with missing details and no photos.

My role

Owned the feature from discovery to launch: scope, PRD, priorities and delivery.

Outcome

3 sources

Suppliers can import from a PDF, an Excel file or their own website.

Goal

Get a new supplier from sign-up to a sellable catalogue without making them retype it.

What suppliers needed

Suppliers already have a catalogue, but in whatever form suits them: a PDF they send to clients, an Excel sheet, or their own website. Products often lack details like descriptions or specifications, and many have no photo at all. Retyping everything into our template was the step that slowed onboarding.

Decisions and trade-offs

  • Accept the formats suppliers already useA PDF catalogue, an Excel sheet or a link to their website. No new template to fill in.Three messy inputs to parse instead of one clean template, which is harder to build and to keep accurate.
  • Fill the gaps instead of rejecting incomplete dataWhen a product is missing information, AI generates it from the data that is available.Generated details can be wrong. We accepted that risk because a near-complete listing the supplier can correct beats an empty one they never finish.
  • Solve missing photos with the SKUIf a product has no image, the system finds one online or generates it, using the SKU and product reference.A found or generated image may not match the exact product. For a marketplace, a close image is still better than a product with no photo.

Result

Shipped in 2026 and available to Okadoo's 800+ suppliers. A supplier can now go from the catalogue they already have to listings ready to publish, without a new template to fill in.

What I'd do differently

I'd define the success metrics before launch, not after. Next I'd track three things: time from supplier sign-up to first published product, how often suppliers edit AI-filled fields, and the share of images found online versus generated.
