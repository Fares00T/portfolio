Okadoo, Maystro Delivery, 2026

Getting a B2B marketplace to pay online

Most of Okadoo's retailers paid cash on delivery. I led the launch of online payments, and now most of them pay digitally.

Problem

Customers paid the driver in cash on delivery, so every order depended on cash collection.

My role

Led the launch end to end: discovery, PRD, prioritisation and delivery with the team.

Outcome

70-80%

of B2B customers now pay digitally instead of cash on delivery.

Context

Okadoo is the B2B marketplace of Maystro Delivery, one of Algeria's largest last-mile delivery companies. Suppliers sell to retailers across the country, and cash on delivery was the default way to pay.

Goal

Move retailers from cash on delivery to paying online, without asking them to change how their business already pays.

What retailers needed

Retailers were not attached to cash. They were attached to the way their business already pays suppliers. Some have a Dahabia card from Algérie Poste, some a CIB card from their bank, and many pay larger orders by bank transfer or cheque.

So the question was never "card or cash". It was how to give every retailer a digital way to pay that matches what they already do.

What we shipped

Five ways to pay, so customers can use the method they already trust.

Dahabia cardAlgérie Poste's debit card
CIB cardInterbank bank card
Bank transferFor larger orders
ChequeStill common in B2B
Okadoo WalletA balance inside the app

Decisions and trade-offs

  • Cover B2B habits, not only cardsBank transfer and cheque sit next to card payments, because business customers pay that way.Transfers and cheques can't be confirmed instantly like a card. We accepted that in exchange for reaching the customers who would never pay by card.
  • Support both Algerian card networksDahabia and CIB together reach customers whichever bank or post office account they use.Two card integrations to build and test instead of one. Supporting only one would have left out a large share of retailers.
  • Add a walletThe Okadoo Wallet keeps a balance in the app, so a retailer can pay for the next order in one tap.Holding a balance for customers adds refund and reconciliation work.

Result

70-80% of B2B customers now pay digitally instead of cash on delivery, where cash on delivery used to be the default. The shift happened in 2026, the same year as the launch.

What I'd do differently

I'd set up tracking of the payment method on every order before launch, not after. With a clean baseline, it is easier to show which method moved retailers off cash and to decide what to improve next.

How the team delivered

I run the team in Scrum with 6 engineers and a product designer. Restructuring sprint planning raised on-time task completion by 30%.

Okadoo's screens and internal data are not public, so this case study uses no screenshots. I'm happy to walk through the details in an interview.
