Okadoo, Maystro Delivery, 2026

Automatic B2B billing, exchanges and refunds

B2B customers need a full paper trail for every order. Okadoo now generates each document automatically as the order moves, including when part of a shipment comes back.

Problem

One order needs several documents for the customer and the supplier, not a single receipt.

My role

Defined the document flow and the return rules, and led delivery across all three sides.

Outcome

5 documents

Generated automatically, from pro forma invoice to credit note.

Goal

Every order should produce the documents customers and suppliers need for their accounts, without anyone creating them by hand.

What customers and suppliers needed

A business buyer needs paperwork for its own accounting: a quote before buying, an invoice to pay, a delivery note to check the goods, and a credit note when something comes back. The supplier needs a purchase order to prepare the shipment. And returns are often partial: one damaged carton, not the whole order.

The document chain

Each step of an order produces the document the next person needs.

  1. Pro forma invoiceTo the customer, so they see the full cost before ordering
  2. Purchase orderTo the supplier, to place the order
  3. Final invoiceTo the customer, as the bill to pay
  4. Delivery noteHanded to the customer with the delivery
  5. Credit noteIssued on a refund or return, for a full or a partial shipment

Steps 1 to 4 follow every order. The credit note only appears when something comes back.

Exchanges and refunds

Returns touch three parties, so we built one flow that all of them share and connected it to credit notes.

CustomerRequests an exchange or a refund on an order
SupplierSees the request and handles the product side
AdminOversees and resolves every case

Decisions and trade-offs

  • Generate documents automatically, not on requestEach document is created when the order reaches its step, so nobody has to remember to issue it.Order statuses have to be accurate, because a wrong status issues the wrong document.
  • Handle partial returns from the startA customer can send back the whole shipment or only part of it. The credit note covers exactly what came back.Line-level returns are more work than whole-order refunds, so v1 took longer. Without them, every partial return would turn into manual work for finance.
  • One return flow for three sidesCustomer, supplier and admin see the same case, which ties refunds and billing together.Three interfaces to design and ship together instead of starting with admin only.

Result

Every Okadoo order now produces its documents automatically, and exchanges and refunds run through one flow shared by customer, supplier and admin.

What I'd do differently

I'd bring finance in even earlier. Billing rules look simple until a partial return meets a discount, and those edge cases are cheaper to settle in the PRD than in QA.
